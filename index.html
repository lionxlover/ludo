    How to Play

    Goal

    Race all four of your tokens from your base, once around the board, up your home column and into the centre triangle. The first player (or team) to finish every token wins.

    On your turn

    Tap the dice (or press Space) to roll. Then tap a highlighted token, or a highlighted destination, to move. A 6 lets you roll again, but three 6s in a row forfeit the turn.

    Leaving the base

    A 6 is required to move a token out of your base onto your coloured start cell (this can be relaxed in Settings).

    Captures

    Landing on a cell occupied by a single opponent token sends it back to its base, and grants you another roll. The four coloured start cells and the four star cells are safe: nobody can be captured there.

    Blockades

    Two tokens of one colour on the same track cell form a blockade. Opponents can neither pass nor land on it. Stacked pairs are marked side by side.

    Home column & finish

    After a full lap you enter your coloured home column. The centre needs an exact roll (a relaxed bouncing mode is available in Settings). Finishing a token grants another roll.

    Modes

    Quick Play drops you (Red) against three bots. Custom Game supports 2-4 human hotseat players, any mix of humans and bots (Easy / Normal / Hard), and a partners mode where Red + Yellow face Green + Blue.
